Temple Registry & Management Portal Requirements Document v1.0
==============================================================

Temple Registry & Management Portal
Detailed Requirements Specification


1. Introduction & Purpose
-------------------------

The Temple Registry & Management Portal is a government digital solution designed to give District Collectors (DCs) and temple authorities across Karnataka a unified, secure, and structured platform to manage temple-related data. This document provides a comprehensive requirements specification covering all functional, technical, and operational aspects of the system.


1.1 Background
--------------

Karnataka is home to over 30,000 registered temples spanning Grade A (major endowments), Grade B (medium endowments), and Grade C (smaller temples). These temples are governed under the Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments Act, and the District Collector plays a central oversight role. Currently, temple information is maintained in fragmented paper records and siloed departmental systems, making it practically impossible for DCs to quickly access reliable data on temple assets, staff, trusts, and geo-location.


1.2 Purpose of the System
------------------------

This system serves two primary user groups:

- District Collector (DC): To search, locate, and inspect all temple data within their jurisdiction — across geographic hierarchies and temple grade classifications.
- Temple Authority: To log in to the portal and proactively share, update, and manage their temple's profile, assets, trust details, and staff records.


1.3 Scope
---------

- Geo-hierarchical temple search: City → District → Taluk → Hobli
- Temple classification by Grade A, B, C
- Temple profile management: address, deity, history
- Trust and board member details
- Employee and contractor records
- Asset declarations submitted to DC
- Secure, role-based web portal for both DC and temple authority users
- Document upload and management capabilities
- Audit trails and reporting

2.3 Glossary

Term            Definition
--------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------
DC              District Collector — the senior IAS officer responsible for district-level administration in Karnataka
Grade A Temple  Major temples with large endowments, significant pilgrim footfall, and high annual income as classified by HR&CE
Grade B Temple  Medium-scale temples with moderate income and community importance
Grade C Temple  Smaller temples with limited resources, often managed by local communities
Hobli           The smallest administrative unit in Karnataka, below Taluk level
Taluk           Administrative division below District level, comprising multiple Hoblis
HR&CE           Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments — the Karnataka government department overseeing temple administration
Trust           The registered legal entity responsible for managing temple affairs and finances
Archaka         The hereditary priest who performs daily worship rituals at a temple
RBAC            Role-Based Access Control — a security model restricting system access based on a user's role
MFA             Multi-Factor Authentication — requiring two or more verification methods to log in
UAT             User Acceptance Testing — final testing conducted by end users before system go-live


3. Functional Requirements
--------------------------

3.1 Temple Search Module (DC Portal)

3.1.1 Geo-Hierarchy Search

The DC portal shall provide a cascading dropdown search that follows the administrative hierarchy of Karnataka:

1. State (pre-selected: Karnataka)
2. City / Town
3. District
4. Taluk
5. Hobli

Requirements:
- Each dropdown shall cascade based on the selection made in the parent dropdown.
- Partial selections shall be allowed — e.g., a DC can search at District level without selecting Taluk.
- All dropdowns shall be populated from a master geo-data table maintained by the Super Admin.
- Search results shall update dynamically without requiring a full page reload.
- Result count shall be displayed prominently before the DC views the list.


3.1.2 Grade-Based Filter

- The system shall allow filtering temples by Grade: Type A, Type B, Type C, or All Grades.
- Grade definitions shall be maintained per the Karnataka HR&CE Act classifications.
- Each search result card shall display the temple grade prominently as a visual badge.


3.1.3 Additional Search Filters

Filter                     | Type          | Description
---------------------------|---------------|-----------------------------------------
Temple Name                | Text Search   | Partial name match / full-text search
Deity Name                 | Dropdown/Text | Primary deity worshipped
Grade                      | Multi-select  | A, B, or C
Trust Registration Status  | Toggle        | Registered / Unregistered
Asset Declaration Status   | Dropdown      | Declared / Pending / Overdue
Year of Establishment      | Range Picker  | From year — To year


3.2 Temple Profile View

3.2.1 Temple Master Information

- Temple Name (primary and alias names)
- Registration Number and Grade (A / B / C)
- Primary Deity and Religious Tradition (Shaivite, Vaishnavite, Shakta, etc.)
- Year of Establishment / Historical Period
- Official Address: Door No., Street, Village/Town, Hobli, Taluk, District, PIN Code
- GPS Coordinates (Latitude, Longitude) with embedded map view
- Contact Person Name, Designation, Mobile, Email
- Temple Photograph (uploaded by temple authority)
- Language(s) of Worship
- Linked Mutts or Sub-Temples (if any)

3.3.1 Trust Information

Data Field                  | Format                   | Mandatory
---------------------------|--------------------------|----------
Trust Name                 | Text                     | Yes
Trust Registration Number  | Alphanumeric             | Yes
Date of Registration       | DD/MM/YYYY               | Yes
Registering Authority      | Text                     | Yes

Trust PAN Number           | Alphanumeric (10 chars)  | Yes
Bank Account Number        | Numeric                  | Yes
Bank Name & Branch         | Text                     | Yes
Annual Income (approx.)    | Currency (INR)           | No


3.3.2 Board Members

- Capture details of all trustees and board members
- Full Name, Aadhaar Number (masked), Designation, Appointment Date, Tenure End Date, Contact Number, Address
- Display current vs. past board members separately
- Meeting minutes upload supported (PDF, max 10 MB)


3.4 Employee & Contractor Module

3.4.1 Temple Staff / Employees

Employee Type: Priest (Archaka), Administrative Staff, Maintenance, Security  
Employee Name: Full legal name  
Employee ID: Internal HR identifier  
Date of Joining: DD/MM/YYYY  
Designation: Role title  
Salary Grade: Pay grade classification  
Contact Details: Mobile, Address  
Status: Active / On Leave / Retired


3.4.2 Contractors & Service Providers

- Contractor Name (Individual or Company)
- GST Registration Number
- Type of Service
- Contract Reference Number and Work Order Date
- Contract Period
- Contract Value (INR)
- Payment Status
- Uploaded contract document (PDF)


3.5 Asset Declaration Module
----------------------------

3.5.1 Immovable Assets
- Agricultural Land
- Temple Complex / Buildings
- Leased Properties
- Other Land Holdings


3.5.2 Movable Assets
- Gold and Silver Items
- Idols and Sacred Artifacts
- Vehicles
- Electronic & Office Equipment
- Financial Assets


3.5.3 Declaration Workflow
- Temple authority submits asset declaration
- DC office reviews and accepts / queries / flags for physical verification
- Digital acknowledgement issued
- Overdue declarations flagged
- Full audit history maintained
