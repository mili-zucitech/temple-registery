1. PURPOSE

   This document maps each user role to their primary Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)
   and the pain points TRM must solve. This is the UX and product design
   reference. All feature prioritization should trace back to these JTBD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. SUMMARY

   5 roles analysed. Each role has 3-5 primary jobs and 3-5 current pain
   points that TRM resolves. These are derived from the requirements document
   and typical government portal user research patterns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. DETAILED CONTENT

   3.1 ROLE: DISTRICT COLLECTOR (DC)

       PRIMARY JTBD:
         JOB-DC-01  "When I need to inspect a temple's financial health,
                     I want to retrieve its latest asset declaration
                     so I can assess if it's compliant."
         JOB-DC-02  "When I receive a submission, I want to review it,
                     annotate concerns, and approve or reject it
                     so the temple has an official record of my decision."
         JOB-DC-03  "When my district has multiple pending submissions,
                     I want to see a prioritized list of overdue ones
                     so I can act on time-sensitive cases first."
         JOB-DC-04  "When a higher authority requests a report on temples
                     in my district, I want to export a formatted summary
                     so I can respond quickly to official queries."
         JOB-DC-05  "When I approve a declaration, I want to generate an
                     official acknowledgement letter
                     so the temple has a legally valid receipt."

       CURRENT PAIN POINTS (pre-TRM):
         PAIN-DC-01  Paper files are stored at District Revenue Office;
                     DC must physically request files to review a temple's
                     history. Takes 1-3 days.
         PAIN-DC-02  No standardized submission format; different temples
                     submit documents in different formats, forcing manual
                     review effort.
         PAIN-DC-03  No alert system; DC staff manually track submission
                     deadlines; overdue submissions are often missed.
         PAIN-DC-04  Generating official letters requires separate word
                     processor workflows; no audit of which letters
                     were issued.

   3.2 ROLE: DC_STAFF

       PRIMARY JTBD:
         JOB-STAFF-01  "When the DC asks for information on a temple,
                        I want to quickly look it up and share a summary
                        so the DC can make decisions without delay."
         JOB-STAFF-02  "When I need to prepare a summary report for the
                        DC's weekly review, I want to export a list of
                        pending submissions so the DC knows what needs
                        attention."
         JOB-STAFF-03  "When a temple authority calls the DC office asking
                        about their submission status, I want to check the
                        current status quickly so I can answer accurately."

       CURRENT PAIN POINTS (pre-TRM):
         PAIN-STAFF-01  No centralized record; DC staff must contact
                        sub-offices or dig through physical files.
         PAIN-STAFF-02  No visibility into submission status in real time;
                        staff had to call field offices for updates.
         PAIN-STAFF-03  Risk of sharing wrong/outdated information; no
                        single source of truth.

   3.3 ROLE: TEMPLE_AUTHORITY

       PRIMARY JTBD:
         JOB-TA-01  "When it is time to submit our annual asset declaration,
                     I want to fill out a structured digital form and
                     attach supporting documents so I can submit to the DC
                     without traveling to the office."
         JOB-TA-02  "When the DC requests clarification on our submission,
                     I want to see exactly what was questioned, amend the
                     relevant sections, and resubmit so I can resolve the
                     query efficiently."
         JOB-TA-03  "When we have a new board member on our trust, I want
                     to update the trust details quickly so our records
                     are always current."
         JOB-TA-04  "When our declaration is approved, I want to download
                     the official acknowledgement letter so we have a
                     legal record of compliance."
         JOB-TA-05  "When I need to update our temple's profile (new deity
                     addition, address change), I want to make the change
                     online without paperwork."

       CURRENT PAIN POINTS (pre-TRM):
         PAIN-TA-01  Annual submission requires physically visiting DC
                     office and often involves long queues.
         PAIN-TA-02  No record of clarification threads; temple authorities
                     receive terse rejection letters with no context.
         PAIN-TA-03  Travel cost and time burden, especially for Grade C
                     temples in remote Hoblis that are far from district HQ.
         PAIN-TA-04  No digital acknowledgement; paper acknowledgements
                     can be lost, creating compliance uncertainty.
         PAIN-TA-05  Updating records requires resubmitting everything even
                     for minor changes (no incremental update).

   3.4 ROLE: SUPER_ADMIN

       PRIMARY JTBD:
         JOB-SA-01  "When a new District Collector is appointed, I want to
                     create their account and assign district scope
                     so they can begin work immediately."
         JOB-SA-02  "When Karnataka reorganizes administrative boundaries,
                     I want to update the geo master data so all district
                     and taluk assignments remain accurate."
         JOB-SA-03  "When a temple authority registers, I want to verify
                     their identity and activate their account so only
                     legitimate representatives access temple data."
         JOB-SA-04  "When an audit query arrives, I want to pull the full
                     audit trail for any user or temple so I can respond
                     within the mandated 48-hour window."
         JOB-SA-05  "When a security incident is suspected, I want to
                     immediately lock a user's account and revoke all
                     sessions so the threat is contained."

       CURRENT PAIN POINTS (pre-TRM):
         PAIN-SA-01  No centralized user management; accounts for district
                     offices managed differently in each district.
         PAIN-SA-02  Geo master data stored in spreadsheets; updates are
                     error-prone and must be manually communicated to field
                     offices.
         PAIN-SA-03  Audit trail is paper-based; responding to audit queries
                     takes weeks of manual file searching.

   3.5 ROLE: AUDIT / EXTERNAL VIEWER (future role, not in v1)

       NOTE: Requirements PDF does not explicitly define this role for v1.
       State Government and Audit Bodies are listed as stakeholders but
       no portal access is defined for them in v1. This is captured as
       a future role.

       ANTICIPATED JTBD (for v2 planning):
         JOB-AUD-01  "When conducting a government audit, I want to view
                      a read-only snapshot of approved declarations for all
                      temples in a date range so I can assess compliance
                      without relying on district offices."

   - JTBD-TA-02 (clarification thread visibility) is in scope for v1;
     see /project-docs/05_workflows_and_states/01_clarification_loop.txt
   - External Auditor role is explicitly deferred to v2

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. OPEN QUESTIONS

   - PAIN-TA-03: Should there be a mobile-friendly (responsive) version
     of the portal to support temple authorities in remote areas?
     Currently: web-only scope.
   - PAIN-DC-03: Should DCs receive daily digest emails summarizing pending
     submissions, or only event-triggered notifications?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. RISKS & EDGE CASES

   - JOB-DC-04 (Export): If a DC exports data for a report and the data
     changes before the report is submitted to superiors, the exported data
     is stale. Consider timestamping exports and including a "snapshot as-of"
     date in all export documents.
   - JOB-SA-05 (Emergency lockout): Must be near-instant (sub-5 seconds).
     Requires refresh token revocation AND JWT validation against revocation
     timestamp on every request.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. ASSUMPTIONS

   - Pain points listed are representative of the intended stakeholder group
     even if formal user research was not conducted for this document
   - All JTBD are addressable through the features defined in the requirements
     PDF within v1 scope
   - SUPER_ADMIN is a technical/administrative role, not a field user; does
     not require mobile-optimized UX
